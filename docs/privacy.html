Evening Bell privacy

Evening Bell stores your goals, tasks, daily habits, check-ins (including mood), focus history, reflections, settings and feedback in ANNA's per-user App storage. The app contains no analytics or advertising integrations.

When you request AI breakdown, ordering or coaching, relevant goal/task details, check-in information and your input are sent to ANNA's model service and the provider configured in your ANNA account. Requests consume your ANNA model quota. The app does not receive or store your model API key.

Use Settings to export a backup, remove mood records, or clear app records. Importing a backup replaces current app records only after your confirmation. Files are processed in the app and saved into your ANNA App storage; they are not sent to the model unless you subsequently request AI features using their content.

Feedback is stored in your own App records and is not automatically sent to the developer. Clearing app data resets active records; ANNA's own logs, retention policies and storage backups remain governed by ANNA. Language preference is stored separately. No cross-app data access is requested.

晚钟隐私说明

目标、任务、每日习惯、报到信息（含心情）、专注记录、复盘、偏好和反馈保存在你个人的 ANNA 应用存储中。应用未接入统计追踪或广告服务。

主动使用 AI 拆分、排序或陪伴时，相关目标任务、报到信息和输入会发送至 ANNA 模型服务及你配置的模型供应商，并使用你的 ANNA 模型额度。晚钟不会获取或保存模型 API 密钥。

可在设置中导出备份、删除心情或清除应用记录。导入备份需确认后才替换当前记录；文件在应用中读取并保存至 ANNA，不会仅因导入而发送给模型。反馈仅保存到个人应用数据，不会自动发送给开发者。

清除数据会重置应用的当前记录；ANNA 自身的日志、保留政策和存储备份由平台管理。语言偏好单独保存。本应用不申请读取其他应用数据的权限。
